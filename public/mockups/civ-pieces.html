Roster

The civilizations going into the game, with the design each piece uses now. Switch a piece's design on its card, or write what should change in the box under it. Copy change requests collects every note together with your design choices, ready to paste into a message.

Game preview

A standard board mid-game: each player is one civilization in the game's player colours, with the designs chosen on the roster and the city markers ticked in City marker.

City marker

One thing every city shares, so it never reads as a settlement. Tick any mix: the board redraws, and so does the Game preview. The chip on each shows settlement and city for two civilizations.

Outlines

An edge around every piece so it stands off the tiles. Pick a colour down the side and a width across the top; the board on the right shows every civilization with it: one settlement, one city and two roads each. Widths are board pixels, before the game zooms.

Board

Conflicts

Roster pieces that read as the same shape on the board. Each one lists its current design and the proposed variants; choose one per piece. The line-up on the sand shows the whole group at board size with your picks, so you can see whether the collision is gone.

Look-alike groups

Roster pieces sorted by the shape they share, including milder overlaps. Stashed civilizations are left out. Pieces show your current designs.

All civilizations

Stash

Civilizations set aside for later. Their pieces and variants stay in this file untouched; they are left out of the roster, the conflict checks and the game preview until you bring them back.
